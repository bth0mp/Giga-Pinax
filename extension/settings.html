Bidding

What a carrier or customs charges on hammer, premium and shipping when a coin crosses a border (UK 5%, Germany 7%). The calculator and a coin’s Bid tab start their Import VAT field at this rate for a house whose currency is not your default. Blank: off.

House premiums

Each house’s buyer premium, the VAT and platform fee it adds, and the increment ladder you copied from its own terms. The calculator fills them in when you choose the house.

Copy or paste house presets

Copy the houses above as text to paste into Settings in another browser, or to hand to another collector. Pasted houses are added as rows to review, a house of the same name is updated, and nothing is kept until you Save settings.

Type cards

Off by default. When on, a card for one catalogue type shows up to three photographed museum coins of that type, loaded straight from the museums’ own servers, which see your IP address as any website you visit does.

How it works

The card asks nomisma.org which museum coins of that type have been photographed, and shows up to three of them with the collection that holds each. Nothing about them is saved, and they are never part of Copy summary. In Firefox they also need the nomisma.org access the popup asks for at its first online lookup; without it no photos appear.

      What this data is used for

      RIC, Crawford, Price, Seleucid Coins, CPE and Newell (Demetrius Poliorcetes) lookups use this local data. Bopearachchi references and any lookup the local data cannot answer go online to numismatics.org and nomisma.org. A card built from this data makes no request of its own, unless Show specimen photos above is on: the English Nomisma.org names for every authority, denomination, mint, material and portrait these records carry travel with the package too, under CC BY 3.0, and ruler names on RIC cards come from the Nomisma snapshot in ric-people.js, which takes precedence over the bundled label file. A concept Nomisma publishes no English name for shows as the identifier the record carries, and none is ever guessed.

      Diagnostics

      The last 50 failures on this device, to paste into a bug report. They are never sent anywhere and are not part of a backup.

      What is kept

      Giga Pinax keeps a list of its last 50 failures on this device, so you can paste it into a bug report: when each happened, on which page, in which part (catalogue lookup, specimen photos, acsearch, CoinArchives, local records, page capture or reminders), what kind of failure with its HTTP status, and the extension version. It never holds a search term, reference, web address, record or page text, is never sent anywhere, and is not part of a backup.
