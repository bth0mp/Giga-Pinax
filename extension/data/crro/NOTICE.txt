Bundled CRRO reference data

Derived from Coinage of the Roman Republic Online (CRRO), American Numismatic
Society.
Source: https://numismatics.org/crro/
Data documentation: https://numismatics.org/crro/apis
Source export: crro.rdf, retrieved 24 September 2026

The source type database and this derived database are available under the
Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0:
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

Source filename, SHA-256, coverage, record counts and conversion date are in
metadata.json. Every RRC type in the export is bundled. The source publication
date is unknown. This bundle excludes monograms, symbols, images and
auction-price data. The converter and editable derived data are provided at
https://github.com/bth0mp/Giga-Pinax .
Conversion instructions:
https://github.com/bth0mp/Giga-Pinax/blob/main/docs/LOCAL-CATALOGUE.md

Nomisma.org concept labels are absent from this export. The English name of
every concept these records carry for an authority, issuer, denomination, mint,
material or portrait is bundled in ../nomisma-labels.json instead, under CC BY
3.0; see ../NOTICE.txt. A concept Nomisma publishes no English name for is left
unresolved rather than labelled from another source.
