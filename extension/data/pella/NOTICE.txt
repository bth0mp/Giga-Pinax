Bundled PELLA reference data

Derived from PELLA, Coinage of Alexander the Great, American Numismatic
Society.
Source: https://numismatics.org/pella/
Data documentation: https://numismatics.org/pella/apis
Source export: pella.rdf, retrieved 17 September 2026

The source type database and this derived database are available under the
Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0:
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

Source filename, SHA-256, coverage, record counts and conversion date are in
metadata.json. Only Price's type numbers are bundled: the export's 2,274 Le
Rider die combinations and 382 PELLA type numbers are left out, because no
reference the extension reads cites either. metadata.json records that count
and the reason. The source publication date is unknown. This bundle excludes
monograms, symbols, images and auction-price data. The converter and editable
derived data are provided at https://github.com/bth0mp/Giga-Pinax .
Conversion instructions:
https://github.com/bth0mp/Giga-Pinax/blob/main/docs/LOCAL-CATALOGUE.md

Nomisma.org concept labels are absent from this export. The English name of
every concept these records carry for an authority, issuer, denomination, mint,
material or portrait is bundled in ../nomisma-labels.json instead, under CC BY
3.0; see ../NOTICE.txt. A concept Nomisma publishes no English name for is left
unresolved rather than labelled from another source.
