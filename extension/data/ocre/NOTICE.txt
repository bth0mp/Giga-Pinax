Bundled OCRE reference data

Derived from Online Coins of the Roman Empire (OCRE), American Numismatic Society.
Source: https://numismatics.org/ocre/
Data documentation: https://numismatics.org/ocre/apis

The source type database and this derived database are available under the
Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0:
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

Source filename, SHA-256, coverage, record counts and conversion date are in
metadata.json. The source publication date is unknown. This bundle excludes
monograms, images and auction-price data. The converter and editable derived
data are provided at https://github.com/bth0mp/Giga-Pinax .
Conversion instructions:
https://github.com/bth0mp/Giga-Pinax/blob/main/docs/LOCAL-CATALOGUE.md

RIC ruler suggestions also use a filtered derivative of Nomisma.org concept
data retrieved through the aggregate getRdf API. Nomisma concepts are available
under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

Nomisma source: https://nomisma.org/
Aggregate API documentation: https://nomisma.org/documentation/apis/
The source snapshot hash, date and inclusion counts are recorded in
ric-people.js and docs/LOCAL-CATALOGUE.md.
