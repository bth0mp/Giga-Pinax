Bundled PCO reference data

Derived from Ptolemaic Coins Online (PCO), American Numismatic Society.
Source: https://numismatics.org/pco/
Source export: pco.rdf (https://numismatics.org/pco/nomisma.rdf), retrieved
24 September 2026

The source type database and this derived database are available under the
Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0:
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

Source filename, SHA-256, coverage, record counts and conversion date are in
metadata.json. Only the types of Catharine Lorber's Coins of the Ptolemaic
Empire, volume I, are bundled: part 1 under its plain numbers and part 2 under
its B numbers. The export's 1,959 Svoronos records are not bundled as
records; the 1,067 links that replace a Svoronos record with the CPE type it
became are kept, as the concordance in metadata.json, which also records the
counts and the reason. The source publication date is unknown.
This bundle excludes monograms, symbols, images and auction-price data. The
converter and editable derived data are provided at
https://github.com/bth0mp/Giga-Pinax .
Conversion instructions:
https://github.com/bth0mp/Giga-Pinax/blob/main/docs/LOCAL-CATALOGUE.md

Nomisma.org concept labels are absent from this export. The English name of
every concept these records carry for an authority, issuer, denomination, mint,
material or portrait is bundled in ../nomisma-labels.json instead, under CC BY
3.0; see ../NOTICE.txt. A concept Nomisma publishes no English name for is left
unresolved rather than labelled from another source.
